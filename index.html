Load a game

Paste moves in standard notation (e.g. @0/ B1+ B2\). Move numbers and other text are ignored. The game continues with your current colour and opponent.

How to play Trax

  • Every tile is the same: one side has crossing straight tracks, the other curved tracks. Each tile carries two red and two white track ends.
  • White moves first. On your turn place one tile next to the existing tiles so that every track it touches matches colour.
  • Forced plays: if your tile leaves an empty space with two tracks of the same colour entering it, a tile is added there automatically to join them, as part of your turn. This can chain.
  • A move is illegal if it would leave a space with three tracks of the same colour entering it. Only legal placements are offered.
  • Win by completing a loop of your colour, or a line of your colour running from one edge of the board to the opposite edge across at least 8 rows or columns. If one turn completes winning paths for both colours, the player who moved wins.
  • 8×8: the board may never exceed 8 tiles in either direction. Loop Trax: only loops win.
  • Moves are recorded as column, row and tile type: columns @ A B C… from the left, rows 0 1 2… from the top, counted from the empty row and column just outside the tiles; + is a straight tile, / and \ are curves.

Controls

  • Arrows mode: click an empty space, use the ⟲ ⟳ buttons (or ← →) to cycle legal tiles, then click the tile (or press Enter) to place it. Esc cancels.
  • Right-click mode: left-click a space to show a tile and keep left-clicking to cycle; right-click to place.
  • Click any move in the list to review that position. Ctrl+Z undoes your last move.
